About Khmer Enterprise

Khmer Enterprise (KE) is established by the Royal Government of Cambodia to work as an entrepreneurial ecosystem builder implementing support programs and directly working with partners and beneficiaries to mobilize, invest, and manage resources for a vibrant Cambodian entrepreneurship landscape.

Our Mission Explore Sectors
Our Goals

Khmer Enterprise (KE) Goals are to mobilize, invest and manage resources, from all legitimate sources, to support the development of a vibrant entrepreneurial ecosystem and to provide financial and nonfinancial support to related entrepreneurial ecosystem builders.

They are entrepreneurs, innovative startups, potential SMEs and partner institutions who participate in promoting entrepreneurial activities driving innovation and value-added creation in Cambodia's economy.

Our Direction
Vision

Khmer Enterprise has a vision to become the national platform for promoting entrepreneurial activities.

Mission

Khmer Enterprise's support in building a vibrant entrepreneurial ecosystem can lead to economic diversification and innovation, increasing productivity and decent work creation.

Four Key Principles:
  • Co-sponsoring entrepreneurial ecosystem building
  • Providing financial and non-financial support to start-ups and SMEs
  • Encouraging growth of the four priority sectors
  • Motivating local and international investors to invest in Cambodia's ecosystem
