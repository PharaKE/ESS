Security & Encryption

How we protect your data

We take your data security seriously. The KE Platform is built with modern security standards to ensure your personal information is protected at all times.

Password Hashing (Bcrypt)

Your password is never stored in plain text. It is processed through the bcrypt algorithm with a strong salt factor before storage. This means even KE administrators and database operators cannot view, recover, or use your password.

JWT Authentication

Once logged in, your session is secured using JSON Web Tokens (JWT). Each token is cryptographically signed with a private key, has a limited lifespan, and contains no sensitive personal data. This prevents session hijacking and unauthorized access.

Zero Admin Visibility

No KE staff member, system administrator, or developer has the ability to see your password. Our access control systems ensure that staff can only view data necessary for their specific operational responsibilities.

Secure Data Transmission (HTTPS)

All data exchanged between your browser and our servers is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security). This protects your information from interception during transmission.
